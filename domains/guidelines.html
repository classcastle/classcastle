Custom Domain Guidelines

Last updated: November 2026

⚠️ Important Notice

The custom domain feature is currently in development. The domain format (e.g., .classcastle.link) may change before the official release. We will communicate any changes to users who have reserved domains.

1. Overview

Classcastle's Custom Domain feature allows teachers to reserve a personalized subdomain for their classroom. This makes it easier for students to remember and access their room URL.

These guidelines outline the rules and expectations for using custom domains on the Classcastle platform.

2. Subdomain Requirements

2.1 Format Rules

  • Subdomains must be between 3 and 63 characters in length
  • Only lowercase letters (a-z), numbers (0-9), and hyphens (-) are allowed
  • Subdomains cannot start or end with a hyphen
  • Subdomains must be unique across all Classcastle users

2.2 Reserved Subdomains

Certain subdomains are reserved for system use and cannot be reserved by users. These include infrastructure domains (www, api, mail), brand-related terms (classcastle, official), and functional routes (login, admin, dashboard). A full list is available in the reservation modal.

3. Acceptable Use

3.1 Educational Purpose

Custom domains are intended for educational purposes only. Teachers should use their domain for legitimate classroom activities and student engagement.

3.2 Professional Standards

  • Domains should not contain offensive, inappropriate, or misleading content
  • Domains should not impersonate other individuals, schools, or organizations
  • Domains should not be used for commercial activities unrelated to education
  • Domains should not violate any applicable laws or regulations

3.3 Content Guidelines

While the domain itself may be neutral, the content hosted on that domain must comply with Classcastle's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

4. Ownership and Transfer

4.1 Ownership

Custom domains are owned by the teacher account that reserved them. They cannot be transferred to other accounts without Classcastle's approval.

4.2 Account Status

If a teacher account is closed or suspended, any associated custom domains may be released after a reasonable notice period.

4.3 First-Come, First-Served

Subdomains are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to these guidelines and Classcastle's discretion.

5. Domain Format Changes

As the Custom Domain feature is in development, the domain suffix (currently .classcastle.link) may change before official release. Users who have reserved domains will be notified of any changes via email and in-app notifications.

We aim to maintain consistency, but reserve the right to modify the domain format for technical, branding, or security reasons.

6. Enforcement

Classcastle reserves the right to revoke or modify custom domains that violate these guidelines. Violations may result in:

  • Warning and request to modify the domain
  • Suspension of the custom domain
  • Account suspension or termination in severe cases

7. Changes to Guidelines

Classcastle may update these guidelines from time to time. Users will be notified of significant changes. Continued use of the Custom Domain feature after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated guidelines.

8. Contact

If you have questions about these guidelines or your custom domain, please contact us at support@classcastle.org.
