About Classcastle

Classcastle is a free classroom management tool that helps teachers guide students to the same webpage instantly. Watch the video below to learn more about how it works:

Classcastle makes it easy for teachers to manage their classroom by:

  • Sending all students to the same URL instantly
  • Creating and managing multiple rooms
  • Tracking student attendance and engagement
  • Keeping a history of URLs pushed to each room
  • Using room templates and shortcuts for quick setup
