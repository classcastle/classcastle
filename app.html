IT Deployment Guide

Share this information with your IT department to deploy Classcastle across your school

01. Extension ID

Share this ID with your IT team. They'll need it to force-install the extension across your school's devices.

Extension ID: acfkgmbemlolnjcnhfokelioeienonoi

02. ChromeOS / Chrome Deployment

Add the extension to your Google Admin console under Device Management > Chrome > Apps & Extensions.

{ "ExtensionInstallForcelist": [ "acfkgmbemlolnjcnhfokelioeienonoi" ] }

03. Edge Deployment (Intune/GPO)

For Microsoft Edge, deploy via Intune or Group Policy using the extension ID.

Registry key: HKLM\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\Edge\ExtensionInstallForcelist Value: 1;https://edge.microsoft.com/extensionstore/acfkgmbemlolnjcnhfokelioeienonoi

04. Firefox Deployment

For Firefox, deploy via Group Policy or Enterprise policies using the extension ID.

{ "policies": { "ExtensionSettings": { "classcastle@classcastle.org": { "installation_mode": "force_installed", "install_url": "https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/downloads/latest/classcastle/latest.xpi" } } } }

05. Network Configuration

Safelist these domains in your web filter or firewall to ensure room connections work properly.

*.supabase.co *.classcastle.org

06. Testing

Before full deployment, test on a single device to ensure the extension appears and can join a test room.

  • ✓ Open the storage for this Classcastle site and use local storage, not session storage.

Step 1 of 5

Welcome to Classcastle

Classcastle helps you guide all your students to the same webpage instantly. No more reading URLs off the whiteboard or helping students find the right page.

Step 2 of 5

Create a Room

Creating a room is simple. Just click "Create new room" in your dashboard. You'll get a 6-digit code to share with your students.

Step 3 of 5

Install Student Extension

Students need the Classcastle extension to join rooms. It's lightweight and works on Chrome, Edge, and Chromebooks. Share the Chrome Web Store link with your IT team for easy deployment.

Students Join Your Room

Students open the extension, enter your room code, and they're in! You'll see them light up green in your dashboard when they join.
