2. What Classcastle does

Classcastle lets a teacher create a room and share a room code. Students join the room with the browser extension. The teacher can then send a web address, and the browsers of joined students open it. The teacher dashboard shows each student's name, online status, and current page address.

Classcastle does not lock a student's browser, block other sites, or monitor anything beyond what is described in our Privacy Policy.

7. The student extension

The extension is installed on a student's browser, either by the student or by a school administrator through a managed policy. When joined to a room, it allows the teacher to open pages in that browser. The extension only requests the permissions it needs to do this (for example storage for the session and tabs for navigation).

Students can leave a room at any time. Teachers can remove students from a room. Schools that deploy the extension or desktop app to managed devices are responsible for informing students and families as their policies and local law require.

9. Messaging

Classcastle includes a messaging feature for classroom communication. Teachers use messaging in the web app, and students use it in the Classcastle desktop app once it is released.

  • How it works. A teacher creates a messaging thread in the web app and gets a shareable URL for it. People take part in the thread by joining it with a code in the app.
  • Who can take part. Anyone who has the thread's URL or code may be able to join it, so treat both like a key. Share them only with the students and staff who should be in the thread, and don't post them publicly. Teachers are responsible for who they share them with and for removing anyone who shouldn't be there.
  • What you may send. You are responsible for what you send. Section 5 applies to all messages. In particular, don't send messages that are abusive, bullying, discriminatory, sexually explicit, threatening, or that share personal information about someone else.
  • Student safety. Teachers must supervise messaging with students as they would any other classroom communication, and should not use it for private, non-educational conversations with students. Students should not share personal contact details, passwords, or other private information in a thread.
  • Not for emergencies. Messaging may be delayed or not delivered, for example if someone is offline. Don't use it for urgent or safety-critical communication.
  • Handling and review. We process messages only to deliver them and operate the Service, and we don't use them for advertising. We don't routinely read messages, but we may review them if we receive a report, need to protect a person or the Service, or are legally required to. How long messages are kept is set out in our Privacy Policy.
  • Moderation. We may remove messages, close a thread, or suspend an account that breaks these Terms.
